Core of Gaming: start from the player fantasy and one primary challenge. Identify the important verbs, the obstacle, the meaningful decision, and how the player learns from the result. Preserve an existing direction; when there is no direction, compare a few distinct small concepts. Describe the smallest complete playable loop, the hook, and how success, failure and another attempt work. Match feedback, progression and art direction to that experience. A prototype's playtest should establish whether choices matter and recovery works.
